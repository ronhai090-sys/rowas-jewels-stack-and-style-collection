## About the Collection

Stack and Style is Rowa's answer to everyday fine jewellery. Not bridal. Not ceremonial.
Just beautifully made pieces in gold and diamonds that fit real life — a morning meeting,
a dinner out, a quiet weekend. You can stack them, layer them, or wear one on its own.
Either way, they hold their ground.

The collection covers three categories:

- **Rings** – stackable diamond bands you can mix across fingers or pile on one
- **Bracelets** – slim bangles and minimalist diamond pieces that sit well together
- **Necklaces** – pendants and chains in different lengths, made for layering

## How to Stack

Rings — Start with one band on the index finger, add another on the middle or ring finger.
Mix widths. Odd numbers work better than even.

Bracelets — Combine the Twin Sparkle with the Fancy Shapes bangle. Add a plain gold 
bangle on the other side to balance the weight.

Necklaces — Layer the Sleek Chain as a base. Add the Emerald Teardrop slightly shorter.
Finish with the Love Charm or Ethereal Bloom at collarbone length.
